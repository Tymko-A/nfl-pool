# House style examples

These are real recaps from previous seasons. The recap writer reads this file every week and
matches the voice. Add more examples over time — the more that are here, the closer it gets.
Keep the best ones; delete any you don't want imitated.

---

## Example 1 — per-entrant format, warm ribbing

Week 7 was a week many of us would like to forget...

A lot of tough losses suffered by pool teams, and with the exception of Andrew (our lone 2-0 this
week) the best any of us could muster was a single win. As such, Andrew claws a game closer to
Stevie in the battle for the pool lead... Still early in the season so a lot can happen...
Including the 3-way race between Jeff, Rose Mary and Tymko to see who will be the first to be
mathematically eliminated from contention!

Andrew had the lone 2-0 weekend with his Steelers beating Jordy's Bengals at Heinz Field and his
Seahawks besting Tymko's G-men 24-7. Andrew sits in second place in the pool and only 1 game back
of Stevie — whose teams still need to take both of their Byes. His match ups next week see him
facing the Lions and Taras's Texans — both of whom are only 3-3, so he could muster 2 wins next
weekend. We'll have to watch...

Adrian had a 1-1 weekend. His Cowboys won a game that it would be horribly embarrassing if they
lost against the bottomfeeding 0-7 49ers, but his Broncos suffered an embarrassment of their own,
getting BLANKED by la Chargers, 21-0. No head to head implications in either game though.

Ivan had a 1-1 weekend, but not the way you'd expect... His Jags beat Larissa's Colts, blanking
them 27-0, but his Rodgers-less Packers struggled against the Saints at Lambeau and started what
could be a long slide to the bottom as the season goes on with their star QB nursing a broken
collarbone. His only solace comes from knowing he has an ever-rare Bye and Bye weekend next week.

Rose Mary had a very surprising 1-1 week. The Ravens weren't able to beat Stevie's Vikings, but
somehow da' Bears held Cam Newton and Jordy's Panthers to only a field goal and won 17-3 in
Chicago. Although she and Tymko are both a game ahead of Jeff who is in last place, Jeff has the
edge on net points so a one game swing could easily see either of them in the bottom.

and finally, Jordy had a weekend he'd like to forget, suffering the lone 0-2 result of week 7. His
Panthers were embarrassed by Rose Mary's Bears, held to a measly field goal, and his Bengals lost a
grudge match divisional game against Andrew's Steelers in Pittsburg. He comes upon two
pool-head-to-heads next week.

## Example 2 — notes-and-takeaways format, clipped and betting-inflected

**Week 12 news / notes / takeaways**

- A highly profitable day for the public/favourites — cover in all 12 games on Sunday. Rare air.
- All the Bills do is break your heart. All 6 losses this year have been by a touchdown or less.
- Finally, at long last, the Steelers outgain an opponent — their 450 yards on offence only took
  49 games in a row and a fresh face at OC to accomplish.

**Week 12 game recaps**

Bill's Pack walked into tha D and picked up a huge upset victory to keep some rather slim playoff

Jordy's Birds has needed to rely on plenty of mental resolve these days to keep digging out of

Adrian's Vikes couldn't hold off a late Bears surge which to some of us couldn't come soon enough
after enduring one of the most interminable contests and favourite to be for dud of the century

## Notes on blending the two

The site's recap uses both: a short takeaways list at the top (Example 2's voice), then one
multi-paragraph write-up per game that goes deeper than either example did (football detail first,
pool consequence second), then a short per-entrant section (Example 1's voice), then a forward look.
Team nicknames — Pack, Boys, Niners, Phins, Birds, Vikes, Bolts, G-men — are encouraged.
